### Sections in order

| # | Section | Purpose | Key content |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | **Header** (sticky) | Brand and navigation | "the BA." logo, anchor links (Services, Approach, About, Events, Insights, Contact), "Book a call" button |
| 1 | **Hero** | Say what it is and who it's for in five seconds; show Sophie | Eyebrow, headline, subheadline, primary and secondary CTA, photo of Sophie speaking |

**Why this order:** GCs arrive sceptical of AI hype. The page earns attention with their problem (3), says what's offered (4) and how it's done differently (5), then proves it (6). Only after that does it ask them to trust the person (7). Events (8) come late on purpose: they are a strength, but the main business is advisory. If events came first, the site could read as an events company. The trust strip (2) puts the strongest proof right under the hero for people who only skim.

Accessibility notes (approximate contrast ratios, verify with a checker once values are final):
- Espresso on ivory: about 15:1 ✓
- Ivory on espresso: about 15:1 ✓
- Sand on espresso: about 7:1 ✓
- Muted on ivory: about 5.5:1 ✓ (body text)
- Taupe on ivory: about 3.8:1, so use it **for large text or decoration only**

### Font pairing

- **Display:** Playfair Display (400, 400 italic, 600). Headlines, pull quotes, stats.
- **Text / UI:** Inter (400, 500, 600). Body, navigation, buttons, labels. *Alternatives:* Source Sans 3, DM Sans.
- **Eyebrows:** Inter 500, uppercase, `letter-spacing: 0.18em`, small size.
- **Note:** loading fonts through Google Fonts or `@fontsource` packages means a new dependency or external request, which needs approval under the project rules. Self-hosting the `.woff2` files in `public/fonts` avoids the package.

### Spacing and layout

- Spacing scale: `4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48, 64, 96, 128` px (`--space-1` … `--space-10`).
- Content max-width: `1200px`. Text measure: max `65ch`.
- Section padding: `clamp(64px, 10vw, 128px)` vertical.
- Alternate section backgrounds: ivory → white → **espresso** (for Approach and Contact) for rhythm.
- Grid: 12 columns on desktop, 4 on mobile, gutter `24–32px`.
- Radius: `0` or `2px`. Sharp edges suit the editorial look. **Assumption.**
- Shadows: none, or very soft. Use hairlines (`1px var(--color-line)`) instead.
- Motion: subtle fades only, all switched off under `prefers-reduced-motion`.
