# One-Page Website Discovery

High-level questions to define the strategy, content, structure and visual direction of a one-page website for The Best Advisory (Sophie Best).

Answer them using the scraped LinkedIn data in `docs/linkedin/`. For every answer:

- Base it on evidence from the data and name the source (profile, post, article, featured item).
- Mark anything that is inferred rather than stated as an **assumption**.
- If the data can't answer a question, say so and add it to Open Questions instead of guessing.

## 1. Business & Offer

- What does the business do, in one sentence a visitor understands in five seconds?
- What are the core services, and which 2 to 3 should the website lead with?
- How do clients engage (advisory projects, workshops and training, keynotes, events, go-to-market support for legal tech vendors)?
- How do the related ventures (Knowledge Nexus, The Wellness Brief for Lawyers, The Legal Table, the Legal AI Practice Lab) relate to The Best Advisory, and which belong on this page?
- Is this site a personal brand (Sophie Best) or a company brand (The Best Advisory)?

## 2. Audience

- Who are the primary and secondary audiences (for example in-house GCs, law firms, legal tech companies, corporates, legal professionals looking for training)?
- Which audience should the page be written for first?
- What problems and pressures do they have, in their own words?
- Which regions matter most (UAE, wider Middle East, London)?

## 3. Positioning & Voice

- What makes this business different from other legal tech and AI consultants?
- What are the 3 key messages a visitor should remember?
- What is the brand personality, based on how Sophie writes and presents herself?
- What tone of voice should the website use, and how does it differ from the LinkedIn tone?
- What recurring beliefs or phrases from her content should shape the copy?

## 4. Proof & Credibility

- What proof can be shown: named clients, years of experience, events and attendance, keynotes, partners, accredited training, published articles, audience size?
- Which proof points are strongest for the primary audience?
- Is any proof missing that the site needs (testimonials, case studies, results)?

## 5. Page Structure & Conversion

- What is the single primary action for visitors (book a call, enquire, join an event, subscribe)?
- What secondary actions, if any, should the page offer?
- Which sections should the page contain, in what order, and why?
- What should the hero headline, subheadline and CTA say? Give 2 to 3 options.
- Which existing LinkedIn content (articles, events, photos, videos) should be reused on the page?

Candidate sections: Hero, Problem, Services, Approach / Process, Proof (clients, numbers, logos), About, Events & Education, Insights, FAQ, Contact.

## 6. Visual Direction

- What should the site feel like (for example premium, approachable, corporate, editorial), based on the brand and audience?
- What do the existing brand assets (logo, LinkedIn banner, profile photo, post graphics) suggest for colour, typography and imagery?
- What role should photography of Sophie and her events play?
- What should be avoided (for example generic AI or tech imagery, a cold corporate look)?

## 7. Open Questions for the Owner

List what the data can't answer and the owner needs to decide, for example: contact method and booking tool, pricing or packages, testimonials, logo permissions for named clients, domain, and languages (English only, or Arabic too).

## Final Output Format

Structure the answer into:

1. **Business Summary**: what the business does, the offer and the value proposition.
2. **Target Audience**: primary and secondary audiences, their needs and pain points.
3. **Positioning & Voice**: differentiators, key messages, personality and tone.
4. **Proof**: strongest credibility points, ranked.
5. **Page Structure**: sections in order, each with its purpose and key content, plus the primary CTA.
6. **Copy Starters**: hero options and a short draft for each section.
7. **Visual Direction**: look and feel, colour and type direction, imagery, and what to avoid.
8. **Design System Starting Point**: proposed colour tokens, font pairing, type scale and core components (buttons, cards, section layout), to document later on a `/design-system` page.
9. **Open Questions**: decisions the owner still needs to make.
